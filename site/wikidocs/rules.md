# Login

Create an account from the header, or play a guest match from Games. Ranked search and custom lobbies need a verified email.

# Games

Games lists bot, training, random, ranked, and custom lobbies. A match opens on the lobby page, then sends you into the canvas when it starts.

# For Honor

For Honor is a duel. Bot and random queues are one or two players. A custom For Honor lobby holds two seats and starts when the second player joins.

# For Ever

For Ever is co-op for up to four players. The host starts the match from the lobby. Extra players can leave the lobby without closing it.

# Tickets

Tickets can be bought with Digipog or PayPal and spent on the site (rename, suggestions over the free cap, grants). A finished For Honor or For Ever match does not spend a ticket and does not change rank.
